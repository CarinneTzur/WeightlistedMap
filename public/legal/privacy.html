Privacy notice

Updated November 22, 2026

Contact Weightlisted at getweightlisted@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Information we use

We process account details, coaching applications, public coach profiles, client training details, favorites, reviews, messages, safety reports, and administrator action records to operate the marketplace. Public coach profiles and published reviews can be viewed without an account. Client profiles and conversations are available to authorized participants under the service’s access rules.

Messages and safety

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorized administrators can review conversations for support and safety. Administrator reviews record the administrator, time, and stated reason. Participants can report concerns; clients can block a coach to stop new messages in both directions.

Service providers and email

We use Vercel to host the app and Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. If you enable email notifications and delivery is configured, an email provider receives your email address and a generic notification. Message text is not included in notification emails. You can change your preference in Account settings.

Retention and deletion

Account information and conversations have no automatic expiration while the account remains active. Deleting your account removes your profile and ordinary associated conversations and applications. Restricted safety reports and administrator action records are retained for support and safety after deletion. These retained records may contain information you provided in a report. Contact us to request review or removal of retained personal information.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, control client profile visibility, block a coach, report a concern, change notification preferences, and delete your account in the app. For access, correction, or deletion assistance, contact our support email.

Uploads and payments

Client message uploads and paid Quick Services are not available in V1. Do not send payment details through messages.
